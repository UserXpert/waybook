## Resolved findings

1. **Client desktop search was too narrow.** The placeholder was visibly truncated beside the primary action. The client-only filter field now has a usable minimum width on desktop and preserves full width on mobile.
2. **Client modal had no effective scrim.** A later shared CSS rule forced the client modal scrim transparent. The modal now restores its intended dimmed, blurred backdrop, keeping the dialog visually distinct from client data underneath.
3. **Client modal primary action lacked a deliberate desktop placement.** Save now aligns to the modal’s trailing edge on desktop; it remains full-width on mobile.
4. **Resource/product drawer titles wrapped badly at 390px once Save is promoted beside Close.** The mobile editor headers now use compact spacing and action padding so “Add resource” / “Add product” remain legible while retaining both actions.
5. **Service drawer used a different primary-action placement.** Its Save action remained at the bottom of the form while comparable drawers place it beside Close. It is now a native form-associated header action, with the same 37.6px action height and compact spacing as the close control at 390px and desktop widths.

## Additional verified checks

- The mobile overview and reports layouts were revisited in dark theme at 390px; their primary cards, summaries and navigation remained usable without overlap.
- The sales detail drawer was opened at desktop and mobile widths in dark theme. Its close control, client/service cards and monetary summary remained readable at 390px.
- The locations drawer and services drawer were opened at 390px. Neither caused page-level horizontal overflow; the services header action/close geometry was also rechecked at 1440px.
- The bookings list was inspected at 390px. Its 820px data table stays inside a local horizontal region rather than widening the page; this preserves the page shell but does not solve high-volume scanning.
- The staff directory/details workspace was opened at 1440px and 390px. Its directory, profile tabs and detail form remained inside the page width; work-hours was intentionally left to its active owner.
- The notifications review table was opened at 390px. Its client, appointment and review action stay within the page width; following the action was intentionally left to the calendar owner.
- A systematic route sweep then covered overview, reports, sales, services (new drawer), locations (new drawer), staff, bookings, notifications and calendar at 1440px and 390px in both directly rendered light and dark modes. The page shell did not develop horizontal overflow in any of those 36 route/viewport/theme checks. This is a layout-screening result, not a claim that every visual detail or data state was exhaustively tested.
- The calendar date picker was reopened at dark/mobile. It exposed the selected-date summary, month/year selectors, navigation and direct day choices. The work-hours recurring-shift dialog was reopened at light/desktop; it exposed close, add-shift, cancel and save controls without making a change.
- The online-booking setup and its launch-preview surface were opened at 390px in dark theme; the selected embed configuration and service chooser remained accessible without a small visual defect found in that state.

## Limitations

- The original screenshot folders supplied for this pass were blocked by macOS temporary-folder permissions, even with an elevated read attempt. Their user-described defects were rechecked against the running local UI where applicable.
- Appointment-editor, calendar-picker and work-hours-dialog combinations were not each exhaustively repeated at every width and in both themes; their rows distinguish route-level coverage from directly opened dialog coverage.
- The browser automation’s persisted theme selection reverted when re-entering Settings, but every light/dark route sweep above checked the rendered `data-waybook-theme` state after navigation. This is an audit-environment limitation, not a product conclusion.
